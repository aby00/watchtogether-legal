Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 31 October 2026

WatchTogether ("we", "our", or "the app") is a movie decision-making platform that helps groups decide what to watch together. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how it is handled.

1. Information We Collect

When you use WatchTogether, we may collect or process:

  • Display Name: The name you enter when creating or joining a room.
  • Room Information: Room codes and information needed to manage shared rooms.
  • Movie Activity: Movies added to a room, your YES or NO votes, and voting results.
  • Technical Information: Basic information required for the app to function, such as device, browser, and connection information, where made available by the services we use.

We do not require you to create a traditional user account to use the current room-based experience.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use this information to:

  • Create and manage movie rooms.
  • Allow participants to join shared rooms.
  • Display movie pools and voting results.
  • Calculate majority decisions.
  • Maintain and improve the app's functionality and reliability.

3. Third-Party Services

WatchTogether uses third-party services to provide its functionality:

Supabase
We use Supabase for backend database services and room-related information, including participants, movies and votes.
Privacy Policy: https://supabase.com/privacy

The Movie Database (TMDB)
We use TMDB to search for and display movie information and posters. Movie search requests may be sent to TMDB.
Privacy Policy: https://www.themoviedb.org/privacy-policy

Movie data and images are provided by TMDB. WatchTogether is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information within a room may be visible to other participants in that room, as necessary for the shared movie-selection experience.

We may rely on third-party service providers to operate the app. These providers process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable terms.

5. Data Storage and Security

Room-related information is stored using our backend service provider, Supabase.

We take reasonable steps to protect information processed through the app. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Information may remain stored for as long as it is needed to provide the service or fulfil operational requirements.

If you would like to request deletion of information associated with your use of WatchTogether, contact us using the details below. We will review and respond to reasonable requests.

7. Children's Privacy

WatchTogether is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us so we can review the request.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app evolves. Any updated version will be made available on this page, with a revised effective date.

Child Safety Standards

Effective Date: 31 October 2026

WatchTogether by Snapverse is committed to maintaining a safe environment for everyone using the platform. We have a zero-tolerance policy towards child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), child sexual abuse material (CSAM), and any activity that exploits or endangers children.

1. Zero-Tolerance Policy

WatchTogether strictly prohibits:

  • Child sexual abuse or exploitation in any form.
  • Creating, uploading, sharing, or distributing child sexual abuse material (CSAM).
  • Grooming, solicitation, or attempts to exploit minors.
  • Content or behaviour that promotes or facilitates the sexual exploitation of children.

2. Reporting Safety Concerns

Users can report suspected child exploitation, inappropriate behaviour, or other child safety concerns by contacting our designated safety contact:

Email: abnanyad999@gmail.com

Reports should include relevant details to help us understand and investigate the concern.

3. Review and Action

We will review reported concerns and take appropriate action where necessary. Actions may include:

  • Removing reported content, where applicable.
  • Restricting access to rooms or platform features.
  • Suspending or terminating access for users who violate these standards.
  • Escalating matters to relevant authorities where legally required.

4. Cooperation with Authorities

We are committed to complying with applicable child safety laws and cooperating with relevant law enforcement and child protection authorities when required.

5. Prevention and Safety

We aim to maintain a platform that does not facilitate child exploitation. We will review and improve our safety practices as the app evolves and additional safety features are introduced.
